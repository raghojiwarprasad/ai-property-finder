# Product decisions and boundaries

## Decisions already made

- D01: This is a personalised AI learning product. All UI, content and measurement should reinforce that identity.
- D02: AI is the subject. Mathematics, statistics, programming, writing or business analysis belong only when they support an AI learning goal.
- D03: The preferred entry is CV upload followed by an AI goal. Describing experience manually is an equally valid alternative. Neither a CV nor an employer is required to learn.
- D04: Background chooses relevant examples and provisional starting checks. Goal chooses destination and depth. Demonstrated work controls progression. These are separate data structures and separate decisions.
- D05: Nationality, school prestige, career gaps and current occupation must not impose an ability ceiling. A nontechnical learner can choose a research goal.
- D06: The first experience contains learning and an application task. Generating a long curriculum is not the activation event.
- D07: The main interface has three destinations: Today, My path and My work. A tutor is available inside the work. Settings and profile are secondary utilities.
- D08: One active primary goal and one recommended next activity keep the experience understandable. Goal changes preserve transferable evidence.
- D09: Assessment is part of learning, introduced through useful tasks. No mandatory long placement exam precedes the first lesson.
- D10: A familiar explanation is the entry point. Learners must eventually apply the concept in another context to demonstrate transfer.
- D11: The product uses explicit evidence states, never a fabricated universal “AI IQ” or an unexplained percentage of expertise.
- D12: Simulations and constrained AI workflow exercises are embedded practice. A general agent builder, live integrations and autonomous actions are excluded from the initial release.
- D13: The curriculum has reviewed concepts, prerequisite rules and assessment contracts. AI can adapt explanations and propose changes within that structure; it cannot invent earned capability.
- D14: Learners can ask for simpler explanations, greater depth, different examples, hints or a quick check. Speed and sophistication can vary within the same person and lesson.
- D15: Personal examples use confirmed facts or clearly labelled hypothetical scenarios. The product must never invent a project and claim the user worked on it.
- D16: Progress records what the learner demonstrated, the conditions, assistance used and recency. Completion and confidence are separate from demonstrated performance.
- D17: Sharing starts with a useful challenge or selected piece of work. CV details, employer details and private learning history are excluded by default.
- D18: Old code is reusable only after an inventory of behaviour, security and dependencies. The rejected interface is not a template to preserve.

## Explicit exclusions

Career graphs; career route recommendations; job or university matching; placement guarantees; salary predictions; LinkedIn-style feeds; recruitment; employer surveillance; an open course marketplace; affiliate ranking; mandatory social profiles; public leaderboards; a general-purpose chatbot; arbitrary autonomous agents; custom model training; native mobile applications at first release.

## Scope assumptions made for this handoff

- Adult, self-directed learners; English first; responsive web; individual accounts. These are working assumptions, not inferred personal preferences.
- First release provides four short, complete outcome bundles: practical AI foundations, AI leadership, AI for marketing, and an applied AI builder introduction. Research foundations begin with a genuinely supported starter milestone; advanced research content expands in a later learning release.
- All five destinations can be selected, but only published milestones may be described as available. Future milestones are visibly marked “Planned”. The product must not sell completion of an unfinished research programme.
- Public positioning can remain “Learn AI, your way.” “Learn AI” is a working interface label. Naming and branding are not resolved here.
- Billing is deferred until learning and repeat use are validated. The billing PRD is provided for a later release and does not gate the core first experience.

## What success would feel like

The learner says: “It understood what I meant, explained this through something I know, noticed exactly where I was stuck, and helped me do something I could not do before.” Each part must be observable in a session rather than asserted by marketing copy.
